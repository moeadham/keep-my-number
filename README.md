# Keep My Number

Keep your old number: port it to Twilio, receive calls on your current phone, and get incoming SMS by email.

Calls ring your phone while the caller hears a connecting announcement. You hear who is calling and press **1** to connect. Declined or unanswered calls get an unavailable message—no voicemail.

Runs on **Twilio Functions + Sync + Conversations/MCS**. Uses AllModels for speech and Cloudflare Email Sending REST for email. No Cloudflare Workers or Durable Objects.
